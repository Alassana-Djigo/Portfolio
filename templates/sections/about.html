The Logic Behind the Code

I am Alassana Joaquim Viriato Djigo — a backend developer focused on building reliable systems with Python, while exploring the deeper layers of infrastructure, networking, and cybersecurity.

My work revolves around the architecture behind software: designing APIs, structuring databases, processing asynchronous tasks, and connecting services into coherent systems. From Django and FastAPI to PostgreSQL, Redis, and Docker, I am interested in how individual components come together to create software that is efficient, maintainable, and resilient.

I approach technology by understanding how systems work, how they communicate, and where they can fail. Beyond application development, I explore Linux, networking, security principles, and cloud infrastructure — from analyzing logs and understanding vulnerabilities to studying how systems can be protected, tested, and hardened. For me, building software is not just about making things work; it is about understanding the entire system well enough to make it work reliably.
